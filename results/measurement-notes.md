# Measurement notes

The final retained campaign ran seven scientific child processes sequentially with one worker. Aggregate suite CPU time was 5.275 s, aggregate suite wall time was 5.290 s, and the maximum child-reported resident set size was 94,508 KiB. Unit-test time and parent orchestration are reported separately in clean-reproduction evidence.

The numbers are observations from this container, not portable performance claims or exact reproduction targets. Scientific equality is defined by CSV rows and declared counts. The environment had no swap at intake; every child was subject to the documented 3 GiB virtual-address, 35 CPU-second, 40 wall-second, and exact-row admission guards.
